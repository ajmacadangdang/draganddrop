How to place a card

  1. Keyboard or screen reader: Choose a card from the menu under a design need.
  2. Select and assign: Select a card in the tray, then select the design need where it belongs.
  3. Mouse: Drag a card to a design need.

Change or remove a match whenever you wish. Once all design needs have cards, choose “Check my thinking” for feedback.
